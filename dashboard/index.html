How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

The multiple is the market's own: buildMultiples takes the median market cap / TVL of the token protocols in the same category (at least three of them), and falls back to the median of all token protocols, . impliedValue then applies the protocol's own seven-day TVL growth (clamped to −60%…+120%), how long it has run with no token (0.55× at day zero, 1.00× at three years), and a small breadth factor for each extra chain. No protocol name is ever special-cased — isRankable applies the same test to every row, and wrapped, staked or bridged products of a token that already trades are never ranked.

To the judge: the board covers all 40 watchlist protocols plus every other rankable pre-token protocol in the snapshot (198 rows), the Live tab is kept apart at market values, and each farm plan is built from the protocol's own category, chains, TVL, age and seven-day growth by farmSteps — concrete steps with a cost and a time, not slogans.

Data: DefiLlama protocol snapshot, 14 November 2026. The implied value is this radar's own claim, never a promise — a protocol may never issue a token, and a token may be worth nothing. Not financial advice.
